# NadirNet

🚧 Work in Progress

NadirNet is an offline-first disaster intelligence platform designed to assist emergency responders during connectivity blackouts.

## Problem

During natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, and earthquakes, internet connectivity is often unavailable. Emergency teams struggle to collect, organize, and prioritize incident reports in real time.

## Solution

NadirNet uses local AI models to analyze incident reports directly on edge devices without requiring cloud connectivity.

The platform enables responders to:

* Register emergency incidents
* Prioritize rescue operations
* Generate situation reports
* Operate in low-connectivity environments

## Planned Features

### AI-Powered Incident Triage

Analyze incoming reports and assign urgency levels.

### Situation Report Generation

Automatically summarize disaster response activities.

### Offline-First Architecture

Continue operating during internet outages.

### Multi-Language Support

Assist responders and affected communities in local languages.
